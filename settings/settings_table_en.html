“Dzhmil” sketch settings (version 1.7)

The settings are constants at the top of the sketch (main.cpp). After changing a value, re-upload the sketch to the controller. Verify every change in real-world tests: reaction-zone and filter parameters directly affect the user's safety.

1. Obstacle reaction zones

ParameterDefaultUnitDescription and effect
CENTER_ALERT_DISTANCE_MM1050mmDistance at which the center sensor starts warning. Higher: earlier warning, but more unnecessary alerts in tight spaces. Lower: less time to react. Keep it above VIBRO_VERY_FAR_BORDER_MM, otherwise the “very far” rhythm is never used.
SIDE_ALERT_DISTANCE_MM900mmSame for the left and right sensors. Decrease it if walls in narrow corridors cause constant alerts.
DIRECTION_GROUP_MARGIN_MM300mmIf several sensors see obstacles within this margin of the nearest one, their motors vibrate together. Higher: several motors vibrate together more often (less precise direction). Lower: only the side of the nearest obstacle vibrates.
ALERT_HYSTERESIS_MM80mmMargin when leaving a zone: the signal stops only when the obstacle has moved beyond the zone border by more than this value. Higher: steadier at the border, but the signal stops later. Lower: the motors may twitch at the zone border.

2. Vibration rhythm by distance

The main cue for the user is the length of the pause: the closer the obstacle, the shorter the pause. The zone borders must decrease: VERY_FAR > FAR > MEDIUM > CLOSE.

ParameterDefaultUnitDescription and effect
VIBRO_VERY_FAR_BORDER_MM850mmBorder between the “very far” zone (beyond this border) and the “far” zone.
VIBRO_FAR_BORDER_MM700mmBorder between the “far” zone and the “medium distance” zone.
VIBRO_MEDIUM_BORDER_MM550mmBorder between the “medium distance” zone and the “close” zone.
VIBRO_CLOSE_BORDER_MM400mmBorder between the “close” zone and the “very close” zone.
PATTERN_VERY_FAR120 / 1200ms (pulse / pause)“Very far” zone: sparse single pulse.
PATTERN_FAR120 / 800ms (pulse / pause)“Far” zone: noticeably more frequent pulses.
PATTERN_MEDIUM140 / 450ms (pulse / pause)“Medium distance” zone.
PATTERN_CLOSE170 / 180ms (pulse / pause)“Close” zone.
PATTERN_VERY_CLOSE300 / 60ms (pulse / pause)“Very close” zone: almost continuous vibration.

3. Sensors and filtering

ParameterDefaultUnitDescription and effect
SENSOR_TIMING_BUDGET_US50000µsDuration of one sensor measurement. Higher: less noise and better performance in sunlight and at long range, but slower updates. Must not exceed SENSOR_CONTINUOUS_PERIOD_MS (50000 µs = 50 ms).
SENSOR_CONTINUOUS_PERIOD_MS70msInterval between measurements. Lower: faster reaction, but more I2C bus load and higher power consumption. Must not be shorter than the measurement time (SENSOR_TIMING_BUDGET_US).
SENSOR_MAX_VALID_DISTANCE_MM5000mmReadings beyond this value are treated as invalid. Normally not changed.
FILTER_WINDOW3pcsNumber of latest measurements in the median filter (odd number: 3 or 5). Higher: more robust against noise, but slower reaction.
FILTER_MIN_SAMPLES2pcsHow many measurements confirm an obstacle. 2 = two in a row (delay ≈ 140 ms). 1 gives the fastest reaction, but a single false measurement can trigger vibration (not recommended). Must not exceed FILTER_WINDOW.
MAX_BAD_READS3pcsHow many invalid measurements in a row are needed before the sensor is considered “empty” (no obstacle). Until then the previous value is kept.
SENSOR_STALL_MS500msIf a sensor delivers no new data for longer than this, it is considered hung and its last value is no longer used.
SENSOR_READ_INTERVAL_MS10msHow often the sensors are checked for new data. Normally not changed.
I2C_CLOCK_HZ100000HzI2C bus speed. 100 kHz is more reliable with long wires; 400 kHz is faster but more sensitive to wiring and interference.

4. Battery monitoring (1S2P)

ParameterDefaultUnitDescription and effect
BATTERY_DIVIDER_R_TOP_KOHM47kΩUpper divider resistor (from battery positive to GPIO34). Must match the installed resistor.
BATTERY_DIVIDER_R_BOTTOM_KOHM100kΩLower divider resistor (from GPIO34 to GND).
BATTERY_CALIBRATION_FACTOR1.00—Calibration factor: multimeter voltage ÷ Serial voltage.
BATTERY_LOW_VOLTAGE3.50V“Low charge” threshold (1 pair of pulses). Voltage of a single Li-ion cell under light load. Must be higher than BATTERY_CRITICAL_VOLTAGE.
BATTERY_CRITICAL_VOLTAGE3.30V“Critical charge” threshold (2 pairs of pulses). Must be higher than BATTERY_CUTOFF_WARNING.
BATTERY_CUTOFF_WARNING3.15V“Very critical charge” threshold (3 pairs of pulses): charge immediately. The TP4056 module protection cuts off the battery only at about 2.4–2.5 V, so the warning is given much earlier.
BATTERY_HYSTERESIS_V0.10VTo return to a better level, the voltage must rise above the threshold by this amount. Prevents warning “flicker” near a threshold.
BATTERY_FILTER_ALPHA0.300–1Voltage smoothing: share of the new measurement. Lower: smoother but slower to react. With measurements every 5 s, 0.30 averages over roughly 15–20 s.
BATTERY_READ_INTERVAL_MS5000msHow often the voltage is measured. A measurement is taken only while the motors are not vibrating.
BATTERY_FIRST_WARNING_DELAY_MS5000msTime after startup (or after the charge level worsens) until the first warning sounds.
LOW_BATTERY_WARNING_INTERVAL_MS60000msRepeat period of the “low charge” warning.
CRITICAL_BATTERY_WARNING_INTERVAL_MS15000msRepeat period of the “critical charge” warning.
VERY_CRITICAL_WARNING_INTERVAL_MS5000msRepeat period of the “very critical charge” warning.
LOW_BATTERY_WARNING_PAIRS
CRITICAL_BATTERY_WARNING_PAIRS
VERY_CRITICAL_BATTERY_WARNING_PAIRS		1 / 2 / 3pairsNumber of pulse pairs for each level: low / critical / very critical.
BATTERY_PAIR_PULSE_ON_MS130msDuration of one pulse within a pair.
BATTERY_PAIR_INNER_OFF_MS110msPause between the two pulses inside a pair.
BATTERY_PAIR_GAP_MS450msPause between pairs.
BATTERY_ADC_SAMPLES16pcsNumber of ADC readings averaged per measurement (with a pause of BATTERY_ADC_SAMPLE_DELAY_MS = 2 ms between them). Higher: less noise, but the program is blocked longer during the measurement (≈ 32 ms).
